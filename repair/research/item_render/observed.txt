Design change, 2026-11-07 (offline; no new runtime report). The previous
approach is retired: submitKatana issued its own tessDraw before the original
0x59f200 selected item resources, and katanaSuppress then hid the vanilla sword
even though no katana pixels existed (user-confirmed invisible first person).
The replacement now draws through the only render path this adapter has ever
produced visible, user-approved geometry with: the shared tessellator +
shaderColor/flag globals + guarded submitVisual draw in the world entity pass
(the China Hat / Trail / Jump Rings pipeline), anchored to the local player.

- Position/orientation come from the local player's look rotation, read via
  three validated chains for this exact EXE (ClientInstance+0x58 holder; the
  published 0x1921DF8/+0x18/+0x58 and 0x1921888/+0x10/+0x58 chains whose
  velocity siblings agree with the adapter-proven player layout). The yaw sign
  is confirmed or flipped once by majority vote against actual movement.
  First person anchors at the render camera, third person at the player's
  hand; the mode is discriminated by camera-eye distance with hysteresis.
- The vanilla sword is suppressed ONLY while a D3D11 occlusion query wrapped
  around the katana batches counted real rasterised samples in the last
  second. A draw call alone is no longer treated as visibility.
- Third-person item identity: the 0x5a22f0 hook is now a pure observer (never
  skips the original), and the cached 0x56cd8e return of 0x59f200 is matched
  against the item-context texture pointer learned from a verified
  first-person draw. A one-second bridge after an F5 switch covers the view
  transition; it never lingers. The earlier S1/S3 zeros are consistent with
  the user's build using the cached branch, not the streaming branch.
- Vertex budget: the full double-sided model (15,732 vertices) is submitted in
  2,048-vertex tessBegin/tessDraw/tessEnd batches with tessIdle checks between
  them, mirroring the proven multi-batch hat wireframe+fill pattern.
- No camera hooks, no input changes, no new game-code hooks beyond the three
  already signature-verified entry points (0x59f300, 0x59f200, 0x5a22f0).

This remains unverified in game: occlusion-verified pixels prove visibility,
not orientation, grip or colour fidelity. One final user check in both views
is still required before calling the replacement working.
